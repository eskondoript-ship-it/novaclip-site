The rules for using NovaClip. Same as the privacy page: written to be read by the people it applies to, most of whom are 13 to 18.

Last updated 27 August 2026 · NovaClip · novaclip.org · Privacy policy

The short version

  • You have to be 13 or over. Under 18, ask a parent before you pay for anything.
  • What you make is yours. We claim no ownership of your videos, photos or writing.
  • Don't use NovaClip to hurt, harass, or impersonate anybody, and don't upload things you have no right to.

  • Almost everything lives in your browser. Clear your data and it is gone — there is no copy on our side.

1. Agreeing to these terms

Using NovaClip means you accept what is on this page. If you do not accept it, that is a fair choice — do not use the site.

NovaClip is an independent project run by the person behind novaclip.org. It is not a company with a legal department, and these terms are not going to pretend otherwise.

2. Who can use NovaClip

You must be 13 or over. The site asks your age once and will not let you in below that. Thirteen is where COPPA draws the line: below it, a service like this one needs verifiable consent from a parent, which NovaClip has no way to obtain and does not pretend to have.

If you are under 18, you may use everything here, but you should have a parent's or guardian's permission before you buy anything. Most countries treat a contract signed by a minor as something a parent can undo, and we would rather you asked first than have that argument later.

Parents: the parent dashboard is where the screen-time limits, the content blocking and the activity overview live.

3. Your account is a code

Saving progress across devices gives you a code, not a username and password. Whoever has the code has the account, so treat it like a key.

Two things follow from that, and both are worth saying plainly:

  • Lose the code and the account cannot be recovered. There is nothing on our side that could prove it was yours, because we never collected anything that would.
  • Share the code and you have shared the account. Anything done with it counts as done by you.

Face sign-in, voice sign-in, the click rhythm and a passkey all unlock this browser on this device. They are not an identity we hold; see the privacy policy for exactly what is stored where.

4. What you make stays yours

Videos, photos, edits, writing, thumbnails, anything you produce here — it is yours. NovaClip takes no ownership of it and no licence to use it, and it is not used to train anything.

It also mostly never reaches us. The editor, the photo tools and the studio run in your browser, and what they make is written to your browser's own storage. That has a cost worth understanding: if you clear your site data, or use a different device, or your browser evicts the storage, the work is gone, and there is no backup on our side to restore it from. Export anything you care about.

5. What you may not do

Short list, and none of it should be a surprise:

  • Nothing illegal, and nothing that harasses, bullies, threatens or sexualises anybody — least of all another young person.
  • Do not upload or edit material you have no right to use, and do not use NovaClip to pass somebody else's work off as your own.
  • Do not use the face, voice or camera features on anybody who has not agreed to it. That includes making somebody appear to say or do something they did not.
  • Do not pretend to be somebody else, or to be NovaClip.
  • Do not attack the site — no scraping at volume, no attempts to break the rate limits, the age gate, the parent PIN or anybody else's account.
  • Do not use the AI to produce anything on this list, or to work out how to.

If you see something here that breaks these rules, the report form reaches a person.

6. The AI, and what it is not

NovaClip's AI features send the text you type to a Cloudflare Worker, which passes it to Google's Gemini API. What comes back is generated text that can be confidently wrong.

It is not medical, legal, financial or safety advice, and the safety tools — the message scanner, the harassment checker — are a second opinion, not a verdict. If something has actually happened to you, tell an adult you trust, or the police. A website cannot do that part.

Certificates are earned, not bought

Paying for a certificate enrols you. The credential itself only issues once you have actually done the work — the exports, the trend scans, the tutor sessions and the coins the pricing page lists. That is deliberate: a certificate you could simply buy would not be worth putting on anything. If you pay and never finish the work, you have paid for enrolment and the credential does not issue.

Renewals and cancelling

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. You can cancel at any time, and you keep what you paid for until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling does not delete anything you made.

Refunds

If you are in the UK or the EU you have 14 days to change your mind about a digital purchase. That right can end early if you have already started using the thing you bought and agreed to that at the time — which is how the law works for digital content everywhere, not a rule we invented.

Beyond that: if something did not work, or you were charged for something you did not mean to buy, email novacliptools@gmail.com and say what happened. Parents of under-18s who find a charge they did not agree to should do the same, and we will refund it.

How payment is taken, honestly

Payments are handled by Stripe. NovaClip never sees or stores your full card number — the site keeps only the last four digits, the expiry and the name, so a saved card is recognisable to you.

The awkward part, stated rather than hidden: where a Stripe payment link has not been configured for a plan, that checkout is a demonstration and no money is taken. The checkout says so on screen when it is in that mode. A simulated purchase is not a purchase, and it does not entitle you to anything.
