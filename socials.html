Safe harbour

If you are not safe right now

Nobody on this site is a professional and this page is not a crisis service. In the UK, Samaritans on 116 123 is free, open all night and does not show on a phone bill. Childline on 0800 1111 is free for under-19s. Outside the UK, findahelpline.com lists the number for your country. If somebody is in danger now, ring 999 or your local emergency number.

The rules here, in full

  • Nobody gets ground down. Pile-ons, nicknames somebody did not ask for, and screenshotting someone to laugh at them all count.
  • No personal details — yours or anybody else's. Not your school, not your address, not your phone number.
  • Swearing and spam are caught by the server and suspend the account for two days.
  • Everything else, a person reads. Report it and a moderator sees it.
  • Moderators keep a log of every action they take, and other moderators can read it.

Blocking somebody is not rude and you do not owe anyone an explanation for it.
