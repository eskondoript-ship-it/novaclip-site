    What has been tried on this browser

    Kept on this device, newest first, the last fifty. Nobody else can see it — and neither can NovaClip, because it never leaves here. A sign-in you do not recognise is worth knowing about, which is the whole reason this list exists.

    What "sign in with your face" means here. It unlocks this browser, the way a password manager uses a fingerprint — it is not a server-side identity, and it cannot prove who you are to anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes the profile, and so does the button in the panel. The voiceprint in particular is honest about being demo-grade: it is good enough to tell you apart from someone else in the room, not good enough to be a lock.
