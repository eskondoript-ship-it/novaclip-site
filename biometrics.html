Your face and voice, on this device

Sign in by looking at the camera or saying a passphrase, then drive the whole site by talking to it. Nothing is uploaded — the profile is a row in this browser's storage, and deleting it is one button.

Face

A 128-number descriptor of your face, from face-api.js running in the tab. Not a photo — the image never leaves the canvas it was drawn on.

Modeltiny detector + 68 landmarks + recognition net
StoredlocalStorage, key nc_bio_profiles
Needscamera permission, and a CDN for the weights

Voice

About 2.5 seconds of speech, averaged into 24 numbers describing the shape of your voice. Sign-in compares how close two of those are.

MethodWeb Audio analyser, log-scaled spectrum
Strengthdemo-grade — a good impersonation can beat it
Storedsame device, same key, never sent

Voice commands

Speech recognition listening for the site's own vocabulary — every page, in 20 languages, plus whatever buttons the page you are on is showing.

APIWeb Speech, Chrome and Edge

Things you can say

A sample. Anything in the rail works, and so does any button on the page you are looking at.

What "sign in with your face" means here. It unlocks this browser, the way a password manager uses a fingerprint — it is not a server-side identity, and it cannot prove who you are to anyone else. Clearing your browser data deletes the profile, and so does the button in the panel. The voiceprint in particular is honest about being demo-grade: it is good enough to tell you apart from someone else in the room, not good enough to be a lock.
