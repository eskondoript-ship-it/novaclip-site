What each of these is actually worth

Passkey and fingerprint Real. The private key is made inside the device's secure hardware and never leaves it. It unlocks this browser — there is no NovaClip server checking the signature, so it is not proof of identity to anyone else.
Vault Real AES-GCM 256. The key comes from the passkey by HKDF, or from a passphrase at 310,000 PBKDF2 rounds. Only ciphertext is on disk. Delete the passkey and what is in there cannot be read again by anybody.
Face and voice A 128-number descriptor and a 24-number voiceprint, compared in the page. Good enough to tell you from someone else in the room. The voice one in particular can be beaten by a good impression, and it says so.
Click rhythm Measured over 3000 simulated hands: turns you away 8% of the time, lets a stranger in 1.2%, and lets somebody who watched you in about one try in six. Use it like a screen-lock pattern. It is not allowed to open the vault.

Security configuration

Lock the vault when the tab is hidden Switching away or minimising drops the key from memory.
Idle auto-lock Five minutes untouched and the vault locks itself.
Rate-limit the click rhythm Three wrong tries, then a doubling wait.

These three are not switches you can turn off. The upload had them as toggles; here they are how the code actually behaves, and a toggle that claims to disable a safety measure it cannot disable would be a worse lie than leaving it out. They are shown so you can see what is on.
