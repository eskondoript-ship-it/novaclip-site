Check a certificate

Every NovaClip certificate carries a number. Type it in and this page says who it was issued to, which certificate it is, and the day it was issued — straight from our own records. If what comes back does not match the document in front of you, the document is not one of ours.

Why a number and not a seal. A certificate is a document, and any document can be copied or edited. What cannot be copied is the number: it is calculated from the holder’s name, the certificate and the date, using a key that exists only on our server. Change the name on a certificate and its number stops matching it. Invent a number and it belongs to nobody. Neither can be worked out from the certificate itself.

What this page does not tell you. Only the three things printed on the certificate. No points, no history, no account. And it cannot tell you that the person holding a certificate is the person named on it — no certificate ever could.
