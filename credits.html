Credits

Every 3D model, animation and sound used in NovaClip, and who made it. Most free game assets are shared under Creative Commons Attribution — you may use them commercially, but only if you credit the creator by name with a link. This page is that credit.

Fill in the blanks before you publish.
I have not written any creator names here, because guessing them would be worse than leaving them out — crediting the wrong person is still a licence breach, and an invented name is one you could not defend. Every row below is filled in from your repo except the parts only the download page can tell you. For each model marked NEEDS ATTRIBUTION:
  1. Click Find it — it searches Sketchfab for that filename.
  2. Open your model's page and copy the author name, the page link and the licence.
  3. Paste all three into the ASSETS list at the bottom of this file.
If a model turns out to be licensed "no derivatives", "personal use only", or "editorial use only", it cannot ship in your game — replace it rather than credit it.
How to add a credit
Open credits.html, find the ASSETS array near the bottom, and fill in the row:

{ file: 'vector_smg.glb', use: 'SMG', title: 'Vector SMG', by: 'CreatorName', url: 'https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/...', lic: 'CC BY 4.0' }

The badge turns green on its own once by, url and lic are all filled in. Mixamo characters and animations are covered by your Adobe Mixamo licence and do not need public attribution — they are listed anyway, because knowing where a file came from is worth having.
