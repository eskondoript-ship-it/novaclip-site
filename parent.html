Get the browser shield
What this does and does not do. The shield reads the text a platform puts on screen — titles, channel names, descriptions, search terms — and hides or blocks what matches. That covers the three ways a child actually reaches bad content: searching for it, being recommended it, and clicking it. It does not watch the video, so a clean title on a bad video can still get through. Any product that tells you a filter is total protection is selling you something that does not exist. Settings stay on the device; nothing about your child's browsing is sent anywhere.

Comment safety scanner Scans the newest comments on your kid's uploads for harassment or bullying. Uses the YouTube connection made in Studio.

How reporting works: flagged comments appear here instantly for the parent. "Report to YouTube" opens YouTube's official reporting flow for that exact comment (YouTube doesn't allow apps to file reports directly — a human must confirm). "Email me this" opens a pre-written alert to your email so there's a record.
