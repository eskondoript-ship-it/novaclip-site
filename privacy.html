Privacy

Most people using NovaClip are teenagers. This page is written to be read by them, not by a lawyer.

Last updated 9 October 2026 · NovaClip · novaclip.org · Terms of Use

The short version

  • There is no advertising on NovaClip, and nothing follows you between websites.
  • There is one measurement tool — Google Analytics — counting which pages get visited. What it sees is set out below, and you can switch it off.
  • An account is a code. You can add a username and password if you want one — we still never ask for your name or your email, and the password never leaves your device.
  • NovaClip no longer has face, voice or typing-rhythm sign-in. Those features have been removed and the data they stored is deleted — see below.
  • Nothing the camera or microphone sees is uploaded. The editor, the selfie studio and the voice changer all work inside the page.
  • Almost everything you make is stored in your own browser, not on a server.
  • We do not sell anything to anybody, because we do not hold anything worth selling.

What is kept on your device

Most of NovaClip works without a server at all. These live in your browser's local storage, on the computer or phone you are using:

  • Your display name, avatar, username and language, if you set them
  • Your progress: levels, streaks, coins, certificates and skill history. A certificate also carries the number it was issued under, so it can be printed and checked
  • Video editor projects and their settings
  • A list of sign-in attempts on this device, so you can see anything you do not recognise. It stays here and is never sent
  • Parent settings: the screen-time limits, and a hashed parent PIN
  • How many minutes the site was actually used on each of the last 120 days — nothing about what was done in them. It is what the parent dashboard's week and month charts are drawn from, and what the daily streak counts: a day counts once it reaches fifteen minutes. It stays on the device and is never sent
  • A video you choose as the background of the site, if you choose one. It is held in this browser's own storage on this device, it is never uploaded, and “Remove” on the Categories page deletes it. There is no server here to upload a video to
  • If you connect a YouTube channel: its name, subscriber count, total views and upload count, read once when you connect. That is what your creator level is worked out from, and what the parent dashboard's earnings estimate uses. It is not published, not on any leaderboard, and signing out of YouTube deletes it
  • The last four digits of a saved card, its expiry and the name on it — never the full number, which is not stored anywhere

No API key of any kind is stored here. NovaClip's AI runs through our own server, so you never have to hand a key to a web page — and a version of this site that asked you to has had that box removed.

Clearing your browser data deletes all of it. There is no copy on our side to restore it from, which is the trade for not holding your information.

Accounts, and why they have no name on them

Signing in to save progress across devices gives you a code — a short string like a link to a shared document. Whoever has the code has the account.

You can also add a username and password on the profile page, so signing in on a new phone is something you remember rather than a code you have to still have. Three things about it, because a password box implies more than this one delivers:

  • The password never reaches our server. Your browser puts it through 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 first and sends only the result. There is nowhere on our side it could be logged or leaked from, because it never arrives.
  • It is not a stronger lock than the code. Both open the same account. It is a second door, not a better one.
  • There is still no email, which means nobody can reset it for you. Forget the password and the recovery code is the way back.

We chose this deliberately. Collecting email addresses and passwords from under-18s means holding data that can be breached, and it means a password a young person has probably reused elsewhere. So we hold neither. What our server stores against your code is the progress you saved and the time you saved it — no name, no email, nothing you did not type in yourself.

One correction, because this page used to say something stronger than the truth. It said the server held "nothing that identifies you". Our leaderboard also has to know who claimed a name, so that somebody else cannot post under it, and for a player with no account that used to be recorded as their IP address — which is information about a person, and it was kept with no end date. That was not what this page promised, so the software changed rather than the promise: the address is now put through a one-way hash with a secret only the server has, so what is stored cannot be turned back into an address, and an anonymous claim on a name expires after 90 days. The addresses stored under the old scheme are replaced the next time each player comes back.

The cost is real and worth saying: lose the code and the account cannot be recovered, because there is nothing to prove it was yours.

On a first visit NovaClip asks you to make a profile. It is a question, not a wall — "have a look round first" is on the same screen, and every tool here works without an account. What an account buys is your progress following you to another device.

Biometrics: removed. The device lock: removed too

NovaClip used to offer sign-in by face, by voice and by typing rhythm. All three have been removed from the site.

They were built to be private and they were: the measurements were taken in the page and stored in your own browser, and nothing was ever uploaded. We removed them anyway. Under UK and EU data protection law, a face or voice measurement used to recognise a particular person is "special category" data — the most strictly protected kind there is — and that is true wherever it is stored, including on your own machine. NovaClip is used by people aged 13 to 18. Holding the most sensitive category of data about children, for a sign-in shortcut nobody needed, was not a fair trade.

The passkey lock has gone as well. For a while what was left of this was a passkey lock on the browser: set one up, and the site asked your device to confirm it was you before any page opened. The passkey itself was never biometric data — the private key is made inside the device's own secure hardware, your phone does the fingerprint check, and nothing of the sort ever reached NovaClip. It was removed for a plainer reason: it locked people out. A device refuses that prompt for a dozen ordinary reasons, and the button that removed the lock was on the Profile page, which was behind the lock.

What happens to data already on your device. If you enrolled a face, a voice, a click pattern or a passkey before these changes, all of it — the measurements, the passkey handle and the encrypted Locker it opened — is deleted from your browser the first time you load a NovaClip page after this update. You do not have to do anything, and you can clear this site's data in your browser settings as well if you would rather not wait. There was never a copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete at our end.

Signing in is unaffected by any of this. That is your account on the server, with a password that never reaches it, and it never went through the lock.

Camera and microphone

NovaClip can use your camera and microphone for the video editor, the selfie studio and the voice changer. Your browser asks permission every time and you can refuse; the rest of the site still works.

None of it is uploaded. The video and the audio are processed in the page and stay in your browser.

There is one exception, and you have to switch it on yourself. The editor checks every clip you drop in — how fast it flashes, whether it is blank — and all of that happens on your device. It can also ask the AI to look at the clip and say whether there is nudity in it, which is more accurate than the check that runs for free. That one sends four still frames, at 512 pixels, to our worker and on to Google's Gemini. It is off until you turn it on, it says exactly that before it sends anything, and you can turn it off again in the same place. Nothing it answers is reported to anybody, no account is flagged, and nothing is ever blocked.

NovaClip used to have voice commands, which relied on your browser's own speech recognition — and in Chrome that sends the audio to Google to turn into text. That feature has been removed along with voice sign-in, so nothing on the site listens to you any more.

Google Analytics, and how to switch it off

NovaClip uses Google Analytics 4 to count how many people visit and which pages they use. It is the only measurement tool on the site and there is no advertising attached to it.

It is switched off from advertising, explicitly. Every page tells Google that advertising storage, advertising identifiers and ad personalisation are denied, and turns off the two settings that would otherwise let a Google Analytics property feed Google's advertising profiles. Nothing measured here can be used to target an advertisement at you, on this site or on any other. Advertising aimed at under-18s using profiling is banned outright in the EU, and we would not want to do it regardless.

What it records: the pages you open and when, roughly where you are (worked out from your IP address, which Google shortens before storing — it is not kept in full), your browser, device type and screen size, and which site you arrived from.

What it cannot record: your name, your email or your account code, because NovaClip never asks for the first two and never sends the third. Nothing you type — not a message you check for scams, not an AI prompt, not a video — reaches it.

Nothing is counted until you say yes. The first time you open NovaClip a box at the bottom asks, with the same weight on both answers, and the default before you touch it is no. Here is what you chose, and you can change it right now — taking permission back has to be as easy as giving it:

Why we are allowed to hold any of it, and for how long

Short, because there is not much to account for:

  • Counting visits happens on your consent, asked for above, and it is the only thing on this site that works that way.
  • Saving your progress, your account and the leaderboard happens because you asked for them — the contract between you and this site, in the legal sense. Without them the feature is the thing that breaks.
  • Rate limits, the moderation checks and the parent PIN are our legitimate interest in the site not being wrecked, and in a space used by minors staying usable.

How long: what is on your device stays until you clear it. A saved account and its progress stay until you ask us to delete them. A claimed leaderboard name held without an account lapses after 90 days. The short-lived counters behind the rate limits expire within minutes. There is nothing on our side we keep "just in case".

Where it goes: our workers run on Cloudflare, and the AI features pass your prompt to Google's Gemini. Both are American companies operating in Europe, so some of this is processed outside the EU under the standard safeguards those companies publish. We send them nothing about you beyond what the feature needs.

No profiling, and no decisions made about you by a machine. Nothing here scores you, ranks you or decides anything about you automatically.

Age

NovaClip is for people aged 13 and over. The site asks your age once and will not let you in below 13. If you tell us you are under 13 we do not keep what you typed — you are simply not let in.

If you are a parent and believe a child under 13 has used NovaClip anyway, email us at the address below and we will remove anything connected to their code.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: clear this site's data in your browser settings. Everything on this device goes.
  • Delete a saved account: email us your code and ask. We will delete what is stored against it.
  • Refuse the camera or microphone: say no to the browser's prompt, or turn the feature off. Nothing else breaks.
  • Ask what we hold: email us with your code. Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to see it, correct it, or have it deleted, and we will answer within 30 days.
  • Take back the consent you gave: the two buttons in the Google Analytics section above, any time, with no reason needed.
  • Complain about us to somebody who is not us: see below.

Security

NovaClip is served over HTTPS everywhere. API keys live on the server and are never sent to your browser — there is no box anywhere on this site that asks you for one. Passwords are put through 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 in your browser and never arrive here at all. The parent PIN is stored hashed. The leaderboard's record of who claimed a name is hashed with a server-side secret. Requests to save progress are rate-limited. No system is perfect, and we would rather say that plainly than claim otherwise.

Who runs this, and how to complain about us

NovaClip is run by one person, in Portugal, as an independent project — not a company with a privacy department. That person is the data controller for everything on this page, and novacliptools@gmail.com reaches them directly. There is no data protection officer, because at this size the law does not require one and pretending otherwise would be theatre.

If you think we have handled your information badly, you can complain to a regulator, and you do not have to go through us first. In Portugal that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) — cnpd.pt. If you live elsewhere in the EU you can complain to your own country's data protection authority instead, and in the UK it is the Information Commissioner's Office. We would rather you emailed us first so we can fix it, but that is a preference, not a condition.

Changes

If what NovaClip does changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. A policy that has drifted from the software is a promise being broken quietly, which is worse than having no policy at all.
