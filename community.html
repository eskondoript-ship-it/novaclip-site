The community

Comments everyone can read, friends you actually agreed to, and small groups with their own feed. The rules are short and they are enforced by the server, not by this page.

Everyone

Anyone on NovaClip can read this.

Two days, no warning. Swearing, abuse aimed at a person, or spamming the feed suspends the account for two days. It is checked on the server when you press post, so the message never appears and clearing your browser does not undo it. There are no strikes to use up — the first one counts.

Your groups

What the server knows. Your comments, who your friends are and which groups you are in live on NovaClip's server, because there is no other way for another person to see them. Your NovaCoins, your videos and everything in the Editor stay on your device as before. Anyone you add can see your display name and picture — that is the whole point of a friends list, and it is worth knowing before you add someone you have not met.
