NovaClip AI

Six tools that each do one job. They give you specifics rather than encouragement, and when something will not work they say so before you spend a weekend on it.

Ask the coach

Three options and a pick, not a paragraph of "stay consistent".

Video director

An idea in, a shot-by-shot plan out — hook, scenes, sound, and a CTR guess.

Clip finder

Paste what was said and it picks the moments worth cutting out.

Title tester

Write a few and it scores them against each other, with the reason.

SEO hub

A description that reads like a person wrote it, plus tags worth having.

Thumbnail lab

Generated at 16:9. Read it at the size of a stamp before you use it.

Where the answers come from. Every tool here goes through the same ncAsk() as the rest of the site: NovaClip's shared key by default, or your own from your profile if you have added one. Nothing you type is stored anywhere except your own history on this device. Check anything it tells you about numbers. It is told not to invent statistics and it mostly does not, but "mostly" is not "never", and a made-up figure looks exactly like a real one.
