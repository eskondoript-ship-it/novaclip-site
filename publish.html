Add a clip, then decide

Drop a video in. NovaClip can plan the edit using the tools it actually has, write the title, description and tags, and hold the whole thing until the time you pick — or you can do any of it yourself. It asks first rather than deciding for you.

1The clip

It stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded by opening it here.

Drop a video here, or click to choose one MP4, WebM or MOV

2Who does what?

Pick per job. You can change your mind on any of them afterwards.

3The edit

A plan you carry out in the Editor. It only ever suggests tools that exist here.

4Title, description, tags

Written for you, or by you. The counters are YouTube's real limits.

5Thumbnail

Grabbed from the clip at the moment you choose, with your text over it.

6When it goes out

Pick a time. Read the note underneath before you rely on it.

Scheduled

What this schedule is, honestly. NovaClip is a website with no server behind it. A website cannot upload anything while its tab is closed — there is nothing left running to do it. So this holds the plan, the title, the description, the tags and the thumbnail together, and when the time comes it reminds you and hands you everything ready to paste into YouTube in one go. What it will never do is tell you a video went out when it did not. If you want a video to publish while you are asleep, upload it to YouTube now and use YouTube's own scheduled publishing in Studio — that runs on their servers, and it works.
